About the Portal

A centralized digital guide to discovering tourism destinations, heritage monuments, and travel tips across India.

Our Mission

The Tourist Attraction Information Service Portal is designed to make destination discovery simple, accessible, and informative for domestic and international travelers. Whether planning a weekend visit to historic landmarks in Telangana or a vacation across India, our portal brings together essential information—including opening hours, entry fees, available facilities, and maps—into a fast and easy-to-use platform.

What We Provide

  • Detailed profiles of historical monuments, temples, hill stations, beaches, and waterfalls.
  • Practical visitor facts such as visiting hours, recommended durations, and accessibility.
  • Interactive location coordination and direct Google Maps directions.
  • Curated travel advice, safety precautions, and packing recommendations.

Key Features

  • Dynamic Search: Instant keyword search across attraction names, cities, and states.
  • Category & State Filters: Filter places by theme or geographic region.
  • Saved Wishlist: Bookmark attractions directly in your browser without signing up.
  • Visitor Helpdesk: Direct inquiry form to submit questions or suggestions.
